      Official YouTube Chapter Rules
      
          
      YouTube only turns description timestamps into progress-bar chapters when your list follows three strict rules. If even one rule fails, the timestamps still work as clickable blue links, but the segmented scrubber bar will not appear.
      
          
        
            
      • Start with 00:00: Your very first chapter must begin at 00:00 (or 0:00). Even if your intro is short, label it something clear like 00:00 Intro or 00:00 Overview.
        • 
            
      • Include at least 3 timestamps in ascending order: You need a minimum of three chapters listed from earliest to latest. Use the Auto-Sort button if you added notes out of order while reviewing a cut.
        • 
            
      • Keep every chapter at least 10 seconds long: Any gap under 10 seconds between consecutive timestamps disables chapters for the whole video. Our builder displays each chapter's duration so short gaps stand out right away.
        • 
          
      

          
      Why Clear Chapter Titles Matter for Google Search
      
          
      Google uses YouTube chapter titles to build Key Moments cards right inside search results. Descriptive titles like 04:15 Fixing Microphone Echo perform far better than vague labels like 04:15 Part 2 because searchers immediately see which segment solves their problem.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      Frequently Asked Questions
      
          
      
            
      
              Why are my YouTube chapters not showing up on my video?
              
      Double-check three things first: the top timestamp is 00:00, you have at least three chapters sorted chronologically, and every chapter lasts at least 10 seconds. If all three pass and chapters still do not appear, check YouTube Studio to confirm automatic chapters are enabled and your channel has no active strikes.
      
            
      

            
      
              Do YouTube timestamps need to start at 00:00?
              
      Yes. Without 00:00 as the very first timestamp in your description, YouTube will not split the video progress bar into chapters. If your first note starts later, use the quick fix button in the validator to insert a 00:00 Intro line.
      
            
      

            
      
              Can I put timestamps anywhere in my YouTube description?
              
      Yes. You can place your chapter block below your main summary, affiliate links, or sponsor notes. Just keep all chapter lines grouped together on consecutive lines with one timestamp per line.
      
            
      

            
      
              How do I format timestamps for videos over one hour long?
              
      Use MM:SS for anything under 60 minutes (such as 00:00 and 54:20) and switch to HH:MM:SS once you cross the hour mark (such as 01:02:15). This generator validates and sorts mixed minute and hour formats automatically.
      
            
      

            
      
              Does this YouTube chapter timestamp generator store my video notes?
              
      No. Everything runs locally in your browser with plain JavaScript. Your timestamps and chapter titles never leave your device.
